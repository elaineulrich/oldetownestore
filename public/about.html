A hometown store built on good food, honest prices, and Christ-centered values.

Welcome to the store

Olde Towne Country Store is a family-run deli, bakery, and bulk food store in the heart of downtown Itasca. Our goal is simple: serve great food, give great service, and make everyone who walks through the door feel at home.

We bake our own breads and sweets, slice our deli meats and cheeses to order, and keep our shelves stocked with the pantry staples our neighbors count on.

Thank you for supporting a local business — we're grateful for every one of you.
